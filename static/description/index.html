FlousFlow WooCommerce Connector

Reliable WooCommerce synchronization for Odoo 19

Connect one or more WooCommerce stores to Odoo with controlled product, stock, customer, and order synchronization. Orders are imported as quotations for human review, while webhooks, polling, retries, and audit logs provide operational traceability.

Products and stock

Bidirectional product flows, native variants, SKU/barcode identity, images, and warehouse stock export.

Orders and customers

Store-scoped matching, guest customer capture, total validation, and draft quotations that are never auto-confirmed.

Safe operations

HMAC webhooks, retry queue, incremental sync watermarks, multi-company configuration, and detailed sync logs.
